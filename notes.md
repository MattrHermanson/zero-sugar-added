# Full Steam Ahead - Brainstorm

Idea: Its 1787 and John Fitch has made the first steamboat.
You **Elijah or Anne Walsh** run a humble shipping business on the
shores of the *Mississippi River*. Business for the *Walsh Trade Company*
is good with people and industry moving west. You know, the steamboat will
run you out of business if you don't do anything. You must satisfy customers,
complete contracts, and make money any way you can to grow you business
before your competitors run you out of business with steamboats.

## Game loop

You start out on the shore at your shipping company w/ a Punt.

You can:

* Accept & complete contracts
* Buy & trade between other river companies
* Transport people where they need to go
* Upgrade your raft/boat
